What these counts mean

Raw search results count Brave/Tavily result entries only. Raw ATS jobs include every captured board entry, including unrelated and duplicate jobs. Deduplication uses company + title; all raw variants are retained with duplicate references and a location-aware comparison key. A representative that matches the US/early-career criteria is preferred over a rejected variant.

Matched jobs pass the deterministic criteria and have not been found invalid. Only verified matched jobs also passed the public listing HTTP check. Unchecked, blocked and timeout links are separate from invalid links. No form was opened in a browser, filled or submitted.

Only dates explicitly supplied as publication dates populate Published UTC. Greenhouse updated_at and Lever created_at are shown separately, not presented as publication dates. Missing publication dates stay unknown. Observed-at is the retrieval time, not the posting time.
